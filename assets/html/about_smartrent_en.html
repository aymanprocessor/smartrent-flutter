About Smart Rent

Smart Rent puts thousands of cars at your fingertips at competitive prices. The app also allows booking a car with a driver and delivering the car to your location.

Who We Are

We are Smart Rent Advanced Communications & IT Company, a car rental platform designed to provide an easy, secure and flexible booking experience. Our goal is to offer a diverse fleet, insurance options, and timely support to serve our customers efficiently.

Our Services

  • Car Reservations: Easy booking via web or app for thousands of vehicles.
  • Driver Service: Option to book a car with a professional driver for comfortable and safe transport.
  • Car Delivery: We can deliver the car to the location you choose.
  • Competitive Pricing: Flexible pricing plans to suit different travel needs.
  • Customer Support: Support available to assist you throughout the booking and after-service stages.

Why Choose Us?

  • Modern and diverse fleet to meet all needs.
  • Fast and secure booking process.
  • Flexible payment and insurance options.
  • Trained and responsive customer service.
